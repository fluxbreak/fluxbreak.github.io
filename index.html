Puzzle break meets pinball in this fun on the go mashup! Break your way through stages of breakable diamond puzzles while wielding special abilities.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email flux.break.game@gmail.com.

I bought Remove Ads, but I'm seeing ads again.

Tap Ad Free in the top-right corner of the main menu, then Unlock to restore your purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. Ads you choose to watch for a reward are still offered.

My scores aren't on the Game Center leaderboards.

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Scores post when a run ends.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Progress, unlocks and settings are stored only on your device.
