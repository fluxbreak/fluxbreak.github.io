Flux Break Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Flux Break ("the game") is made by Adam D. Levin ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own: I don't receive your personal information unless you email me.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, the initials you enter on the high-score boards, your progress, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me. Deleting the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the game asks (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and crash or performance diagnostics.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying "Remove Ads" stops the ads that play between stages. Ads you choose to watch for a reward are still available.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple Game Center, your best scores are posted to the game's online leaderboards with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. I never see your payment details. The game only learns whether you own "Remove Ads", so it can turn the ads off and restore the purchase.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and I don't share them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I'll help remove it.

Your choices

  • Deny or change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop between-stage ads.
  • Sign out of Game Center to stop posting scores online.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
